# CoolieApp 🧳

**Uber for coolies** — connect passengers with nearby coolies (porters) for hassle-free luggage assistance.

CoolieApp is a ride-hailing-style platform but for porters instead of cars. Passengers can instantly request a coolie to help carry luggage at airports, railway stations, hotels, bus stands, and more — just like hailing an Uber.

## ✨ Features

- **Instant Booking** — Request a coolie in seconds with a single tap.
- **Live Tracking** — Track your coolie's location in real time as they arrive.
- **Nearby Matching** — Automatically matches you with the closest available coolie.
- **Fare Estimates** — Transparent, upfront pricing based on distance and luggage.
- **Ratings & Reviews** — Rate your experience and help build trust in the community.
- **Secure Payments** — Cashless, in-app payments for a smooth checkout.
- **Multi-Location** — Airports, railway stations, hotels, bus terminals, and more.

## 🚀 How It Works

1. **Open the app** and set your pickup location.
2. **Request a coolie** — choose the number of bags and get an instant fare estimate.
3. **Get matched** — a nearby coolie accepts your request.
4. **Track & Meet** — follow their live location and meet them at the pickup point.
5. **Pay & Rate** — pay securely in-app and rate your coolie afterward.
