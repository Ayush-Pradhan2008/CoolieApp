Frequently asked questions

How much does a coolie cost?

Fares are shown upfront and depend on the number of bags, distance and location. Typical trips start at ₹50, and you see the exact price before confirming.

Where is CoolieApp available?

Airports, railway stations, bus terminals and hotels across 120+ cities and hubs — with new locations added every month.

Are the coolies verified?

Yes. Every coolie completes an ID verification and background check, wears a uniform badge, and is rated after each trip.

How do I pay?

Cashless in-app payments — UPI, cards or wallets. Cash is also accepted if you prefer.

Can I schedule a pickup in advance?

Absolutely. Book up to 24 hours ahead for flight or train arrivals, and your coolie will be at the gate when you land.
